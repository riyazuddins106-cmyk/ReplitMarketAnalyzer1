# MLAI MARKET LANGUAGE BRAIN

## MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT FOR REPLIT AI

You are the principal software architect and research engineer responsible for developing **MLAI — Market Language Brain**.

Your job is NOT to build a conventional BUY/SELL trading predictor.

Your job is to evolve the existing MLAI project into a scientifically controlled system that can **read market price behavior as a language, understand its current meaning, compare it with historical experience, reason about competing scenarios, quantify uncertainty, and explain the market state in clear human language.**

The final system must behave more like an experienced market analyst reading a chart than a simple classification model.

---

# 1. CORE OBJECTIVE

MLAI must eventually accept:

* OHLCV market data
* Instrument
* Timeframe
* Historical context
* Any other legitimately available market information

and produce an evidence-based explanation of the current market state.

The desired output is NOT:

```text
BUY
SELL
BUY = 72%
```

The desired reasoning chain is:

```text
Observation
→ sequence interpretation
→ market structure
→ location
→ context and regime
→ volatility and momentum
→ comparable historical situations
→ historical outcome distribution
→ probability and uncertainty
→ conflicting evidence
→ alternative scenarios
→ confirmation conditions
→ invalidation conditions
→ human-language explanation
```

The system must describe what the available data supports.

It must never invent:

* hidden orders
* institutional intentions
* exact trader intentions
* exact stop locations
* unavailable market-depth information
* certainty that does not exist in the data

---

# 2. MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

MLAI is a **Market Language Brain**, not a prediction-only engine.

A candle is not automatically a signal.

For example:

```text
Hammer ≠ BUY
Engulfing ≠ BUY
BOS ≠ BUY
CHoCH ≠ BUY
Bullish candle ≠ BUY
```

Instead, MLAI must ask:

```text
What happened before this candle?
Where did it happen?
What structure exists?
What is the current regime?
What is volatility doing?
What is momentum doing?
What sequence is developing?
Has price confirmed the interpretation?
What historical situations are comparable?
What happened in those historical situations?
How strong is that evidence?
What evidence disagrees?
What would confirm the interpretation?
What would invalidate it?
```

Patterns are descriptions, not automatic trading commands.

# 3. NON-NEGOTIABLE SCIENTIFIC RULES

These rules must be enforced throughout the entire codebase.

## Rule 1 — Evidence is not certainty

Historical repetition increases or decreases evidence.

It never guarantees repetition.

Never output certainty merely because historical frequency is high.

---

## Rule 2 — No pattern-to-signal shortcut

Never implement:

```text
hammer → BUY
engulfing → BUY
BOS → BUY
CHoCH → SELL
```

Every observation must be interpreted using context.

---

## Rule 3 — No invisible facts

From OHLCV alone, MLAI cannot claim:

```text
institutions are buying
institutions are selling
smart money entered here
exact stop-loss locations
hidden liquidity exists at an exact price
buyers are definitely defending this level
```

Allowed language:

```text
Price behavior suggests rejection of lower prices.

Price repeatedly reacted near this area.

The movement is consistent with a failed downside break.

```

---

## Rule 4 — Explicit causality

Every feature must have a known availability timestamp.

A feature may only be used after all information required to calculate it would actually have been available.

Examples:

```text
Current candle anatomy
→ available after candle close

ATR
→ available after required candles close

Confirmed swing
→ available only after confirmation candles close

Future return
→ target only

Future high/low
→ target/evaluation only
```

Never allow future information into the evaluated market state.

---

## Rule 5 — Future outcomes are targets only

Never use:

* future return
* future high
* future low
* future MFE
* future MAE
* future structural event
* future confirmation

as input features for the state being evaluated.

---

## Rule 6 — Locked test must remain untouched

A locked unseen period must NEVER be used for:

* feature selection
* representation design
* threshold tuning
* model selection
* retrieval configuration selection
* memory construction
* explanation tuning
* acceptance decisions

---

## Rule 7 — Accuracy is not the main success criterion

Prioritize:

1. Calibration
2. Generalization
3. Stability
4. Uncertainty quality
5. Evidence traceability
6. Scenario quality
7. Robustness across regimes
8. Baseline comparison

Accuracy is only one metric.


```text

```

For every holdout candle:


Never reveal future outcome information before the prediction is recorded.
