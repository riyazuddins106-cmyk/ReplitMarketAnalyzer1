MLAI CANDLE LANGUAGE — MASTER PROJECT SPECIFICATION
===================================================

VERSION: 1.0
DATE: 2026-08-20

PURPOSE
=======

The purpose of this project is to build MLAI as a market-language intelligence
system.

The central idea is:

    A candlestick is not merely a number.

    A sequence of candlesticks represents a language.

Just as human language has:

    alphabet
    -> letters
    -> words
    -> phrases
    -> sentences
    -> grammar
    -> meaning
    -> context
    -> understanding

MLAI should have:

    OHLC data
    -> candle anatomy
    -> candle vocabulary
    -> candle patterns
    -> candle sequences
    -> market structure
    -> context
    -> market meaning
    -> historical experience
    -> prediction
    -> human-language explanation

The final objective is for MLAI to behave like an experienced market analyst
who can read a chart, understand what the candles are communicating, explain
what is happening, identify possible future scenarios, and provide
probabilities based on historical experience.

IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE
===================

The system must NOT simply memorize future market data and then pretend to
predict it.

MLAI must learn chronologically.

The fundamental learning cycle is:

    OBSERVE
       |
       v
    UNDERSTAND
       |
       v
    PREDICT
       |
       v
    REVEAL FUTURE
       |
       v
    COMPARE PREDICTION WITH REALITY
       |
       v
    LEARN FROM RESULT
       |
       v
    CONTINUE

This cycle must be enforced throughout testing.

===================================================
1. THE FOUR MAJOR KNOWLEDGE COMPONENTS
===================================================

The system should distinguish four different types of information.

1. RAW MARKET DATA
------------------

File:

    market_data.bin

Purpose:

    Store the actual market candles.

Contains, where available:

    timestamp
    open
    high
    low
    close
    volume
    instrument
    timeframe
    source metadata

This is the market's actual historical record.

It must remain traceable to the original candle.

No derived interpretation should destroy the original OHLC information.


2. CANDLE LANGUAGE KNOWLEDGE
----------------------------

File:

    candle_language.bin

Purpose:

    Store the foundational knowledge required to understand candlesticks as
    a language.

This is equivalent to the teacher's textbook.

It should contain definitions and relationships for:

    candle anatomy
    OHLC
    body
    upper wick
    lower wick
    range
    bullish candle
    bearish candle
    neutral candle
    doji
    hammer
    inverted hammer
    shooting-star-like structures
    marubozu-like structures
    engulfing structures
    inside bars
    outside bars
    rejection
    compression
    expansion
    momentum
    hesitation
    continuation
    reversal conditions

It must also contain knowledge about sequences and structures.

Examples:

    higher high
    higher low
    lower high
    lower low
    trend
    range
    breakout
    breakdown
    BOS
    CHoCH
    support
    resistance
    liquidity/context
    volatility
    momentum

The knowledge book is not the same as historical experience.


3. HISTORICAL EXPERIENCE
------------------------

File:

    market_experience.bin

Purpose:

    Store what MLAI has actually learned from chronological historical
    experience.

This is equivalent to the teacher's years of experience.

Example:

    Pattern:
        hammer-like candle

    Context:
        after a strong downtrend near a recent low

    Historical observations:
        1000 examples

    Outcomes:
        continuation upward: 63%
        continuation downward: 25%
        neutral: 12%

This is experience, not a definition.

===================================================
2. CANDLE AS A LANGUAGE
===================================================

The system must treat the candle as the smallest meaningful unit.

A candle contains:

    Open
    High
    Low
    Close
    Volume (if available)
    Time
    Instrument
    Timeframe

From this MLAI derives candle anatomy.

Example:

    Open  = 100
    High  = 110
    Low   = 95
    Close = 108

MLAI should understand:

    bullish candle
    relatively large body
    lower wick
    upper wick
    total range
    body-to-range relationship
    rejection characteristics
    volatility characteristics

The numerical representation may remain available to the machine.

The system must also produce a human-readable representation.

Example:

    "The candle closed strongly above its open, showing buying pressure.
     Price rejected lower levels during the candle and finished near the
     upper part of the range."

Technical representation and human-language representation must coexist.


===================================================
3. CANDLE VOCABULARY
===================================================

The knowledge system should recognize candle vocabulary such as:

    bullish
    bearish
    neutral
    doji
    hammer-like
    inverted-hammer-like
    shooting-star-like
    marubozu-like
    long upper wick
    long lower wick
    small body
    medium body
    large body
    compression
    expansion
    rejection
    momentum

The exact classification must be based on measurable OHLC relationships.

MLAI must not claim that a named pattern automatically guarantees a future
outcome.

A candle pattern is an observation.

Its predictive meaning depends on context and historical experience.


===================================================
4. SEQUENCE LANGUAGE
===================================================

One candle is equivalent to a word.

Multiple candles form a sequence.

Example:

    bullish large candle
        ->
    small bearish candle
        ->
    doji
        ->
    bullish expansion candle

This becomes a sequence.

The system should be able to translate it into something like:

    "Strong buying pressure was followed by a small pullback and then
     hesitation. The subsequent bullish expansion suggests that buyers
     regained control."

The sequence should be represented both technically and in human language.


===================================================
5. MARKET STRUCTURE
===================================================

MLAI must understand structural relationships.

Examples:

    HH = Higher High
    HL = Higher Low
    LH = Lower High
    LL = Lower Low

Example:

    HH -> HL -> HH -> HL

Human translation:

    "The market is maintaining bullish structure because price continues
     to form higher highs while pullbacks are holding above previous lows."

Example:

    LH -> LL -> LH -> LL

Human translation:

    "The market is maintaining bearish structure because rallies are failing
     at lower levels and price continues to form lower lows."


===================================================
6. STRUCTURAL TRANSITIONS
===================================================

MLAI should recognize transitions such as:

    bullish structure weakening
    bearish structure weakening
    breakout
    failed breakout
    BOS
    CHoCH
    trend continuation
    potential reversal
    range formation

Important:

A possible reversal is not the same as a confirmed reversal.

Example:

    HH -> HL -> HH -> failure -> LH

Human interpretation:

    "The previous bullish structure is weakening. Price failed to establish
     another higher high and has formed a lower high. This indicates a
     possible structural transition, but confirmation is still required."


===================================================
7. CONTEXT
===================================================

A candle cannot be interpreted correctly in isolation in every situation.

MLAI must consider context.

Context may include:

    recent trend
    recent swing structure
    support
    resistance
    range position
    volatility
    momentum
    previous candle sequence
    breakout status
    pullback status
    market regime
    location relative to recent highs/lows

Example:

The same hammer-like candle can mean different things:

    near major support
    -> potentially meaningful rejection

    in the middle of a range
    -> weaker information

    directly below major resistance
    -> continuation may be less reliable

Therefore:

    candle pattern != automatic prediction

Instead:

    candle pattern
        +
    context
        +
    structure
        +
    historical experience
        =
    conditional interpretation


===================================================
8. KNOWLEDGE VS EXPERIENCE
===================================================

This distinction is fundamental.

KNOWLEDGE:

    "A hammer-like candle has a relatively small body and a long lower
     rejection wick."

EXPERIENCE:

    "In this specific context, historical hammer-like candles produced
     bullish continuation 67% of the time."

Knowledge tells MLAI what something means.

Experience tells MLAI how that thing behaved historically.

The final system needs both.

===================================================
10. WALK-FORWARD TESTING
===================================================

At every prediction point:

    STEP 1
    ------
    Read only candles available up to current time.

    STEP 2
    ------
    Translate the current candles into market language.

    STEP 3
    ------
    Consult historical experience that existed BEFORE the prediction point.

    STEP 4
    ------
    Generate prediction.

    STEP 5
    ------
    Record prediction.

    STEP 6
    ------
    Reveal the future candle(s).

    STEP 7
    ------
    Compare prediction against actual result.

    STEP 8
    ------
    Add the newly revealed experience to experience memory.

    STEP 9
    ------
    Move to the next candle.

This process repeats continuously.


===================================================
11. FUTURE LEAKAGE RULE
===================================================

This is a NON-NEGOTIABLE rule.

At candle T:

Allowed:

    candle T
    candle T-1
    candle T-2
    all previously observed candles

Not allowed:

    candle T+1
    candle T+4
    candle T+8
    candle T+16
    any future information

Future candles are outcomes.

They are not input features.

Example:

    At 10:00

MLAI may use:

    09:55
    09:50
    09:45
    ...
    10:00

It may NOT use:

    10:05
    10:10
    10:20

when making the 10:00 prediction.


===================================================
12. PREDICTION
===================================================

MLAI should predict future behavior probabilistically.

Example:

    H+4

    bullish continuation: 67%
    bearish continuation: 22%
    neutral: 11%

Human interpretation:

    "Historical experience moderately favors bullish continuation over the
     next four candles. The evidence is not certain and bearish/neutral
     outcomes remain possible."


===================================================
13. PROBABILITY RULE
===================================================

MLAI must NOT automatically output:

    98%
    99%
    100%

just because a pattern looks strong.

Probability must come from evidence.

If there are only five historical examples, MLAI must communicate that
the evidence is weak.

Example:

    "Only five comparable historical cases were found. The estimated
     probability is therefore unreliable."

A high probability should require sufficient evidence and stable historical
support.


===================================================
14. HISTORICAL EXPERIENCE
===================================================

Every revealed prediction result can become experience.

Example:

    State:
        bullish structure
        hammer-like candle
        near recent low
        normal volatility

    Prediction:
        bullish = 70%

    Actual:
        bearish

The system records:

    prediction was wrong

and updates the experience associated with that state.

Over thousands of observations, the system develops conditional statistics.

This is the equivalent of professional experience accumulation.


===================================================
15. EXPERIENCE SHOULD BE CONTEXTUAL
===================================================

MLAI should not merely count:

    "hammer = bullish"

It should learn:

    hammer
    +
    trend
    +
    location
    +
    volatility
    +
    structure
    +
    sequence
    +
    market regime

Example:

    Hammer at support after downtrend
    -> bullish continuation historically 71%

    Hammer in middle of range
    -> bullish continuation historically 49%

    Hammer below major resistance
    -> bullish continuation historically 34%

The system therefore learns contextual behavior.

===================================================
26. TESTING STRATEGY
===================================================

Testing must occur in stages.

TEST 1 — FILE DISCOVERY

Verify that all relevant datasets are found.

PASS:

    expected files discovered.

FAIL:

    important dataset cannot be located.


TEST 2 — DATA VALIDITY

Verify:

    timestamp order
    duplicates
    OHLC
    missing values
    instrument
    timeframe

PASS only when the data is valid or every exception is explicitly documented.


TEST 3 — KNOWLEDGE BOOK

Verify:

    candle_language.bin exists
    binary can be reopened
    version is correct
    required vocabulary exists
    definitions are retrievable

PASS:

    knowledge can be loaded without corruption.


TEST 4 — LANGUAGE TRANSLATION

Feed known candles.

Verify:

    numerical candle
        ->
    technical representation
        ->
    human-language interpretation

PASS:

    interpretation is consistent and traceable.


TEST 5 — CAUSALITY

For a selected timestamp T:

    remove/hide all future candles.

Run prediction.

Then reveal future candles.

Verify that prediction output before revelation is identical to what would
have been produced without future access.

PASS:

    no future information was available.


TEST 6 — CHRONOLOGICAL LEARNING

Run:

    learn
    predict
    reveal
    update

and verify that experience count increases only after outcomes are revealed.


TEST 7 — WALK-FORWARD TEST

Train/learn on earlier data.

Predict later unseen data.

Compare:

    prediction
    versus
    actual result


TEST 8 — EXPERIENCE IMPROVEMENT

Measure whether accumulated historical experience improves prediction.

Do NOT assume that it must improve.

Measure it.

Compare:

    baseline
    versus
    knowledge-only
    versus
    knowledge + experience


TEST 9 — CALIBRATION

Measure:

    Brier score
    log loss
    calibration
    confidence reliability

Only after retrieval/representation quality has been validated.


TEST 10 — UNSEEN PERIOD

Reserve a completely unseen period.

MLAI must not learn from it until predictions have been recorded.

This is the strongest test of chronological integrity.

    "Does MLAI retrieve genuinely comparable market states?"

Not:

    "Can we make the similarity score higher?"

A mathematically similar candle is not necessarily a market-state equivalent.

    observation
    interpretation
    historical evidence
    prediction
    certainty

    "Price formed a doji."

    "Bullish continuation is currently the favored scenario."

    "The historical edge is modest."

40. THE CORE PHILOSOPHY
===================================================

The final MLAI should not simply answer:

    "BUY"

or:

    "SELL"

It should understand and explain the market.

It should be able to say:

    "This is what the candles are currently communicating."

    "This is the structure being formed."

    "This is the context."

    "These are the historical situations that behaved similarly."

    "This is what happened in those situations."

    "Therefore this scenario currently has the strongest historical support."

    "These conditions would confirm it."

    "These conditions would invalidate it."

    "This is the uncertainty."

The goal is not to manufacture certainty.

The goal is to build a machine that progressively develops:

    KNOWLEDGE
        +
    EXPERIENCE
        +
    CONTEXT
        +
    REASONING
        +
    EXPLANATION

41. FINAL DEVELOPMENT RULE
===================================================

DO NOT optimize for an impressive result.

DO NOT change K until accuracy improves.

DO NOT manipulate historical windows until the result looks good.

DO NOT use future information.

DO NOT assume a pattern guarantees an outcome.

DO NOT manufacture probabilities.

DO NOT guess unknown binary formats.

DO NOT replace existing MLAI components before auditing them.

Instead:

    inspect
    verify
    measure
    test
    document
    then modify

Every major stage must have a PASS / FAIL / REVIEW result.

The objective is a reproducible, auditable Market Language AI.
