name: Price-anchored interpreter
description: Human-readable MLAI explanations must anchor every market claim to observed prices and causally confirmed levels.
---

The user wants candlestick language translated into English with explicit current OHLC, support/resistance price zones, test counts, rejection evidence, historical probabilities, confirmation, and invalidation prices.

**Why:** General statements such as “near resistance” were not understandable without identifying which price level and which candles supported the claim.

**How to apply:** Keep the explanation layer separate from preserved v4.x forensic engines; use confirmed causal swings and report zones plus evidence, never unsupported certainty or hidden buyer/seller intent.
